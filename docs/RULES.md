# Dream Read: the learner's rules (read this first, every session)

These are the product rules the learner (native Chinese speaker, about B2, Intel MacBook Pro) has given.
When code and this file disagree, this file wins. Change it only when the learner changes a rule.

## Listening mode: the stop at a hard word or phrase (latest rule, 19 Oct)
1. Listening reads the book. It stops at every word **or phrase** above the learner's level, and at every
   yellow "learning" word, unless it was already explained within the last 500 words.
2. At the stop: say the word (or phrase) **3 times**.
3. Then **wait 5 seconds** with the microphone listening. Nothing else is said.
   - "**I know**" → mark it known (saved for good, highlight removed, never explained again). No explanation.
     Continue reading **from the current sentence** (read it again from its start), to the end of the book.
   - "**bye bye**" → the AI says nothing more. No explanation. Continue reading **from the current sentence**.
   - Anything else (the learner repeats the word, says a question, or is silent) → after the 5 seconds the AI
     explains the word in this exact context; if the learner said something, the explanation also answers it.
     The learner's pronunciation is often transcribed badly: never ask them to repeat; work out what they
     probably meant from the book's context.
4. After the explanation, wait 5 seconds again with the microphone listening:
   - "**I don't know**" → explain again, deeper and slower, from a different angle. Then this window again.
   - "**I know**" → mark known, continue from the current sentence.
   - "**bye bye**" or nothing → the word stays "learning" (yellow); continue reading **from the current sentence**.
5. Reading always resumes from the **start of the sentence that was interrupted**, never the next sentence.
6. Easy controls: big "I know" and "I don't know" buttons appear during every 5-second window, plus keys
   **K** = I know, **D** = I don't know, **Esc** = bye bye (continue). The microphone opens by itself in these
   windows; "Always on" keeps it open all the time.

## Phrases
- Phrases of 2 to 5 words (idioms, phrasal verbs, fixed expressions, slang) are found per chapter, each with a
  CEFR level decided **before** they are shown. Above-level phrases are highlighted and explained exactly like words.

## Explanations
- Easy English, plain spoken prose, no headings, no labels, no lists.
- **No filler**: never say things like "we already know this", "the Chinese translation is", "let me explain",
  "great question", "as I said". Start teaching at once and go step by step.
- Explain the meaning **in this exact context** of the book; paint the scene in the learner's mind with words
  (never images).
- Chinese: only the two meanings that fit this sentence, spoken at the end by a **Chinese voice** (never by an
  English voice); 中文 on/off button.
- Never say the word "stop" to the learner.

## Reading mode
- Single tap = I don't know (card + spoken explanation, then the voice waits). Double tap = I know.
- The card, translation and explanation disappear by themselves when the voice finishes.

## Saving
- Every explained word goes to My Vocabulary (+ Documents/Dream Read/my-vocabulary.csv). Known words stay known.
- All settings, styles, prompts, keys and progress persist across restarts.

## Working agreement
- Build in small steps; send a build after 2 or 3 features; the learner tests on the Mac the same day.
- Before a new feature, repeat the understanding back in 3 to 5 lines.
- The learner's Mac is Intel: always check Intel (x64) binaries and speed.
