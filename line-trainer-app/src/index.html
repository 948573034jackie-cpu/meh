🔗 Follow my Spotify
The app sees what Spotify is playing through Last.fm (free, works with free Spotify). One-time setup:
  1. Make a free account at last.fm/join.
  2. Open last.fm/settings/applications → Spotify Scrobbling → Connect, and log in to Spotify.
  3. Get a free API key at last.fm/api/account/create (any app name, e.g. “Line Trainer”; the other fields can stay empty). Copy the API key.
  4. Fill in both boxes below and tap Start following.
